# Day 51 – Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 51** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built **Product Discovery & Sprint Planning**, an **AI-powered product strategy and agile planning platform** that helps teams transform ideas into actionable product roadmaps. The application supports product discovery, user story creation, sprint planning, backlog prioritization, and AI-driven planning recommendations to streamline software development.

By combining AI with Agile methodologies, the platform enables product managers and development teams to prioritize features, estimate effort, define sprint goals, and deliver value more efficiently.

# 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the product discovery process.
* Convert product ideas into actionable user stories.
* Prioritize product backlogs using AI insights.
* Plan sprint goals and development tasks.
* Learn Agile and Scrum planning workflows.
* Improve collaboration and project execution.
