Day 10/60 – Claude AI Mastery Challenge
Build Your Personal Portfolio Website

Overview

Today, I built my own Personal Portfolio Website to showcase my skills, projects, achievements, and learning journey.

A portfolio website is more than just an online resume—it acts as a personal brand, helping recruiters, clients, and collaborators understand who you are and what you can build.

This project helped me learn how to structure a professional online presence while applying modern web design principles.

📚 What I Learned
🔹 What is a Portfolio Website?

A portfolio website is a personal digital space where you can showcase:

Skills
Projects
Achievements
Certifications
Experience
Contact Information

It serves as a central hub for your professional identity.

🎯 Why Every Developer Needs a Portfolio

A resume tells people what you know.

A portfolio shows people what you can do.

Benefits:

✅ Builds credibility

✅ Demonstrates real-world skills

✅ Showcases projects

✅ Improves personal branding

✅ Helps recruiters evaluate work

✅ Increases job opportunities
