# Day 28 – Build a Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## 📌 Overview

On Day 28 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I built a **Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator** using Claude AI.

Hospital admission is one of the most important stages in a patient's healthcare journey. Before treatment begins, hospitals must verify patient information, insurance coverage, required documents, medical history, and admission readiness.

This project simulates the complete hospital admission process through an interactive dashboard, helping users understand how hospitals prepare patients for admission while ensuring accurate documentation and efficient workflows.

Build an AI-powered Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator that helps users:

* Assess patient admission readiness

# 🏥 What is Hospital Admission Readiness?

Hospital Admission Readiness is the process of ensuring that every requirement is completed before a patient is admitted for treatment or surgery.

This includes:

* Patient Registration
* Identity Verification
* Insurance Validation
* Medical History Review
* Consent Documentation
* Clinical Assessment
* Final Admission Approval

---

# 🔄 Admission Workflow

## 1. Patient Registration

Collect:

* Patient Name
* Age
* Contact Details
* Emergency Contact
* Identification Documents

---

## 2. Insurance Verification

Verify:

* Insurance Provider
* Policy Status
* Coverage Eligibility
* Benefits
* Authorization Status

---

## 3. Medical Assessment

Review:

* Diagnosis
* Medical History
* Allergies
* Current Medications
* Vital Signs

---

## 4. Required Documentation

Confirm availability of:

* Government ID
* Insurance Card
* Physician Referral
* Medical Reports
* Consent Forms

---

## 5. Admission Readiness Check

AI evaluates whether all admission requirements have been completed.

Possible Results:

🟢 Ready for Admission

🟡 Additional Information Required

🔴 Admission Delayed

---

## 6. Final Admission Decision

Generate:

* Admission Status
* Missing Requirements
* Estimated Admission Time
* Recommendations
