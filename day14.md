# Day 14 – Detect Job Red Flags Before You Apply with Claude

## Overview

On Day 14 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I learned how to use Claude AI to identify potential job red flags before submitting an application.

Not every job posting is a great opportunity. Some positions may have unrealistic expectations, unclear responsibilities, poor compensation, or signs of a toxic work environment. Claude can help analyze job descriptions and highlight warning signs that might otherwise be overlooked.


## Objective

Build an AI-powered workflow that evaluates job postings and identifies potential risks before applying.

The goal is to:

* Save time by avoiding low-quality opportunities.
* Make more informed career decisions.
* Focus on roles that align with skills, goals, and expectations.


## How Claude Helps

Claude can review job descriptions and identify:

✅ Unrealistic skill requirements

✅ Excessive responsibilities for a single role

✅ Vague job descriptions

✅ Poorly defined compensation details

✅ Signs of overwork or burnout culture

✅ Lack of growth opportunities

✅ Misalignment between title and responsibilities


## Common Job Red Flags

### 1. Unrealistic Requirements

Example:

* Entry-level position requiring 5+ years of experience.
* Expectation to master multiple specialized domains simultaneously.

### 2. Vague Responsibilities

Example:

* Duties are unclear.
* No specific goals or expectations mentioned.

### 3. Missing Salary Information

Example:

* No compensation range provided.
* Benefits and perks are not mentioned.

### 4. "Wear Many Hats" Culture

Example:

* One role expected to handle development, marketing, sales, support, and operations.

### 5. Excessive Workload Indicators

Keywords such as:

* Fast-paced environment
* Must handle pressure
* Work hard, play hard
* Available after hours

May indicate poor work-life balance.

### 6. No Growth Path

Example:

* No mention of learning opportunities.
* No clear career progression.


## Sample Prompt

Analyze the following job description as an experienced career coach and hiring manager.

Identify:

1. Potential red flags
2. Missing information
3. Work-life balance concerns
4. Career growth opportunities
5. Overall job quality score out of 10

* 5+ years of experience

### Claude's Findings

|                      |                                                   |
| Experience           | Too high for a junior role                        |
| Scope                | Responsibilities appear unrealistic               |
| Salary               | Not mentioned                                     |
| Work-Life Balance    | Flexible hours may indicate overtime expectations |
| Growth Opportunities | Not clearly defined                               |

Recommendation:
⚠️ Research Further Before Applying
