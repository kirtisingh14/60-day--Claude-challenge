# Day 24 – Build Your Startup Business Strategy Report

## Overview

On Day 24 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I learned how to build a **Startup Business Strategy Report** using Claude AI.

A great startup idea and MVP are only the beginning. Long-term success depends on having a clear business strategy that defines how the company will acquire customers, generate revenue, compete in the market, and scale sustainably.

This project focused on transforming a startup concept into a structured business strategy document that can guide decision-making and execution.


# 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered Startup Business Strategy Report that helps founders:

* Define strategic goals
* Analyze the market
* Develop competitive positioning
* Create a growth plan
* Build revenue strategies
* Identify risks and opportunities
* Prepare for scaling

---

# 🚀 Why Business Strategy Matters

Many startups fail because they:

❌ Lack a clear business model

❌ Focus only on product development

❌ Ignore customer acquisition

❌ Underestimate competition

❌ Fail to plan for growth

A business strategy aligns vision, execution, and growth.
