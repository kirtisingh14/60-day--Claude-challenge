# Day 17 – Build an AI Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

On Day 17 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I learned how to build an **AI Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard**.

The purpose of this project is to help vehicle owners, buyers, and fleet managers understand the true cost of vehicle ownership by analyzing fuel expenses, maintenance costs, mileage efficiency, and yearly operating expenses.

Using AI, users can gain valuable insights into spending patterns and make smarter transportation decisions.


# Objective

Build an AI-powered dashboard that analyzes:

* Fuel consumption
* Monthly fuel expenses
* Cost per kilometer
* Maintenance expenses
* Vehicle efficiency
* Annual ownership cost

The dashboard transforms raw vehicle data into actionable insights.


# 🚗 Problem Statement

Many vehicle owners focus only on the purchase price while ignoring long-term ownership costs.

However, the actual cost of owning a vehicle includes:

* Fuel expenses
* Maintenance costs
* Insurance
* Registration fees
* Repairs
* Depreciation

An AI-powered dashboard can help visualize and optimize these expenses.


# Dashboard Features

## 1. Vehicle Profile

Store details such as:

* Vehicle Name
* Vehicle Type
* Fuel Type
* Purchase Price
* Year of Purchase

Example:

Vehicle: Tata Nexon

Fuel Type: Petrol

Purchase Price: ₹10,00,000

## 2. Fuel Cost Analysis

Calculate:

* Monthly Fuel Cost
* Annual Fuel Cost
* Cost per Kilometer
* Mileage Efficiency

Inputs:

* Distance traveled
* Fuel price
* Mileage

Output Example:

Monthly Distance: 1,500 km

Mileage: 18 km/l

Fuel Price: ₹100/l

Monthly Fuel Cost:
₹8,333

## 3. Maintenance Analysis

Track:

* Service costs
* Oil changes
* Repairs
* Tire replacements
* Unexpected expenses

Benefits:

* Identify cost trends
* Predict future maintenance expenses

## 4. Annual Ownership Cost

Calculate total yearly expenses:

Annual Cost =

Fuel Cost +
Maintenance +
Insurance +
Registration +
Miscellaneous Expenses

Provides a complete ownership cost overview.


## 5. Cost Comparison

Compare:

* Petrol vs Diesel
* EV vs Petrol
* EV vs Diesel
* Multiple vehicles

Example:

| Vehicle          | Annual Cost |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| Petrol Car       | ₹1,20,000   |
| Diesel Car       | ₹95,000     |
| Electric Vehicle | ₹55,000     |


## 6. AI Insights

Generate recommendations such as:

✅ Most economical vehicle

✅ Cost-saving opportunities

✅ Fuel efficiency improvements

✅ Long-term ownership analysis

✅ Vehicle replacement suggestions


#  How AI Helps

The AI assistant can:

* Analyze fuel spending patterns
* Detect unusual expenses
* Predict future costs
* Compare vehicle options
* Generate personalized recommendations

Example Prompt:

Analyze my vehicle expenses and suggest ways to reduce annual ownership costs.


# Sample User Input

Vehicle: Tata Nexon

Fuel Type: Petrol

Monthly Distance: 1500 km

Mileage: 18 km/l

Fuel Price: ₹100/l

Monthly Maintenance: ₹1,500

Insurance: ₹18,000/year
