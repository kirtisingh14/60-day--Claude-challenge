# Day 30 – Build a Supply Chain Optimizer

## Overview

Welcome to **Day 30** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Supply Chain Optimizer** that helps businesses analyze and improve their supply chain operations. The application demonstrates how AI can optimize logistics, inventory, transportation, supplier selection, and overall operational efficiency through data-driven recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand supply chain optimization concepts.
* Analyze inventory and transportation data.
* Optimize supplier selection and procurement strategies.
* Reduce logistics costs while maintaining service quality.
* Improve delivery performance using AI recommendations.
* Visualize operational KPIs through an interactive dashboard.

## ⚙ How It Works

1. Generate or load supply chain data.
2. Analyze supplier performance.
3. Review inventory levels.
4. Optimize transportation routes.
5. Identify operational bottlenecks.
6. Generate AI-powered recommendations.
7. Review optimization dashboard.
8. Compare before-and-after performance metrics.
