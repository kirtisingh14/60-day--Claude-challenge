# Day 26 – Build a Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator

##  Overview

On Day 26 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I built a **Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator** using Claude AI.

Prior Authorization (PA) is a critical process in healthcare where insurance providers review and approve medical treatments, procedures, or medications before they are covered. This project demonstrates how AI can streamline and simulate the end-to-end authorization workflow, helping healthcare professionals understand approval processes, identify bottlenecks, and improve operational efficiency.

---

# 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered simulator that models the complete Prior Authorization workflow, including patient information, insurance verification, medical necessity review, approval decisions, and workflow analytics.

The simulator helps users visualize how healthcare authorization systems work and where AI can improve efficiency.

---

# 🏥 What is Prior Authorization?

Prior Authorization is a process used by health insurance companies to determine whether a prescribed treatment, medication, or medical procedure meets coverage requirements before approval.

The process typically involves:

* Patient Information
* Insurance Verification
* Clinical Documentation
* Medical Necessity Review
* Approval or Denial Decision
* Appeals (if required)
