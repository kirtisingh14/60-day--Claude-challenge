📚 What I Learned
🔹 What is an AI Resume Optimizer?

An AI Resume Optimizer is a system that analyzes a resume and provides recommendations to improve:

ATS compatibility
Keyword matching
Resume structure
Professional language
Recruiter readability
Job-specific alignment

The goal is to increase the chances of getting shortlisted for interviews.

🎯 Why Resume Optimization Matters

Most companies use ATS software to filter resumes before they reach recruiters.

A resume may be rejected because:

❌ Missing keywords

❌ Poor formatting

❌ Weak project descriptions

❌ Lack of measurable achievements

❌ Unclear skills section

AI helps identify these issues and suggest improvements automatically.
