# Day 25 – Build an AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview

On Day 25 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I built an **AI Shark Tank Simulator** using Claude AI.

This project simulates a real-world startup investment pitch where AI takes on the role of experienced investors (Sharks) and evaluates a startup idea from multiple business perspectives.

Instead of receiving generic feedback, the simulator analyzes the startup's business model, market opportunity, financial projections, scalability, competition, and founder readiness before delivering an investment decision.


# 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered Shark Tank simulator that helps founders:

* Practice startup pitching
* Validate business ideas
* Receive investor-style feedback
* Identify business risks
* Improve investment readiness
* Prepare for real investor meetings


# 🦈 What is an AI Shark Tank Simulator?

The AI Shark Tank Simulator recreates the experience of pitching a startup to a panel of investors.

The AI evaluates the business using common venture capital criteria and provides:

* Investment decision
* Startup score
* Founder feedback
* Business improvement suggestions
* Negotiation insights



# 🚀 Startup Evaluation Framework

## 1. Startup Overview

Analyze:

* Startup idea
* Problem statement
* Proposed solution
* Target customers
* Market opportunity

## 2. Founder Evaluation

Assess:

* Domain expertise
* Passion
* Vision
* Execution capability
* Founder-market fit

Example Score:

| Category   | Score |
| ---------- | ----- |
| Vision     | 9/10  |
| Execution  | 8/10  |
| Leadership | 8/10  |


## 3. Market Opportunity

Evaluate:

* Total Addressable Market (TAM)
* Growth potential
* Customer demand
* Industry trends


## 4. Business Model

Review:

* Revenue streams
* Pricing strategy
* Profitability
* Unit economics

---

## 5. Competitive Analysis

Compare:

* Existing competitors
* Competitive advantages
* Product differentiation
* Market positioning

---

## 6. Financial Assessment

Analyze:

* Revenue projections
* Burn rate
* Profit margins
* Funding requirements
* Break-even timeline

---

## 7. Scalability

Review:

* Technology scalability
* Team expansion
* Customer acquisition strategy
* Global opportunities

# 💰 Investment Decision

Possible outcomes:

🟢 **Invest**

Strong market potential with scalable business fundamentals.

🟡 **Conditional Investment**

Promising idea but requires improvements before funding.

🔴 **Pass**

Business requires significant validation or refinement.

# 🎤 Shark Feedback

Each AI investor provides feedback on:

* Product
* Business model
* Customer validation
* Competitive advantage
* Revenue strategy
* Risks
* Growth opportunities

Example:

> "Your product solves a real problem, but I would like to see stronger customer validation and clearer revenue projections before investing."

### Investors Look Beyond Ideas

A great idea must be supported by execution, validation, and a sustainable business model.

### Numbers Matter

Revenue, market size, and financial planning are critical for attracting investors.

Customer feedback and traction significantly improve investment potential.
