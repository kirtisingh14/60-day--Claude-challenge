Day 7/60 – Claude AI Mastery Challenge
Choosing the Right Claude Model & Effort Level

Overview

Today, I learned how to use Claude more effectively by understanding when to use Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus, and how different effort levels impact output quality, speed, and depth.

One of the biggest mistakes people make is using the same model and effort level for every task.

The key is matching the right model and right effort level to the specific task.

⚠️ Biggest Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Using Opus for simple tasks

❌ Always using Low Effort

❌ Writing vague prompts

❌ Asking multiple unrelated questions together

❌ Expecting perfect outputs without refinement

❌ Not providing sufficient context

💡 Key Takeaways
The best AI results come from choosing the right model.
Most users can accomplish 80% of their work using Sonnet.
Higher effort levels should be reserved for important tasks.
Better context leads to better outputs.
Prompt quality and model selection work together.

🚀 Day 7 Reflection

Today, I realized that becoming productive with AI is not just about writing better prompts—it’s also about selecting the right model and effort level for the task.

The combination of Context + Prompt + Model + Effort determines the quality of the final output.

Learning how to make these choices can dramatically improve productivity and results.
