# Day 27 – Build a Prior Authorization Story Simulator

## 📌 Overview

On Day 27 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I built a **Prior Authorization Story Simulator** using Claude AI.

Unlike a traditional workflow simulator, this project focuses on the complete patient journey by presenting Prior Authorization as an interactive, scenario-based experience. Users follow a patient case from diagnosis through insurance review, approval or denial, appeals, and final treatment outcomes.

The simulator helps users understand the real-world challenges faced by patients, healthcare providers, and insurance companies while demonstrating how AI can improve healthcare communication and workflow efficiency.

---

# 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered story simulator that allows users to experience the Prior Authorization process through realistic healthcare scenarios.

The simulator aims to:

* Explain the Prior Authorization lifecycle
* Simulate patient journeys
* Demonstrate approval and denial scenarios
* Teach healthcare workflow concepts
* Improve healthcare process awareness

---

# 🏥 What is a Prior Authorization Story Simulator?

A Prior Authorization Story Simulator is an interactive educational application that presents healthcare authorization as a step-by-step story.

Instead of simply showing workflow stages, users make decisions, review documentation, respond to payer requests, and experience different outcomes based on their choices.

---

# 📖 Story Flow

## 1. Patient Visit

Scenario:

A patient visits a healthcare provider with symptoms requiring specialized treatment.

Information Collected:

* Patient Details
* Medical History
* Diagnosis
* Insurance Information

---

## 2. Physician Recommendation

The provider recommends:

* Medication
* Medical Device
* Diagnostic Test
* Surgical Procedure

The simulator explains why Prior Authorization is required.

---

## 3. Documentation Stage

Required Documents:

* Clinical Notes
* Diagnosis Codes
* Lab Reports
* Treatment History
* Physician Recommendation

Users learn why each document is important.

---

## 4. Insurance Review

The insurance provider evaluates:

* Medical Necessity
* Policy Coverage
* Clinical Guidelines
* Supporting Evidence

Possible outcomes:

🟢 Approved

🟡 Pending Information

🔴 Denied

---

## 5. Appeal Scenario

If denied, users can:

* Review denial reasons
* Upload additional documentation
* Submit an appeal
* Track appeal progress

---

## 6. Final Outcome

Possible endings:

✅ Authorization Approved

⏳ Additional Review Required

❌ Final Denial

🎉 Treatment Successfully Begins
