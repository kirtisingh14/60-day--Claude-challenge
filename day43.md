# Day 43 – Build AI Workflow Architect

##  Overview

Welcome to **Day 43** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built **AI Workflow Architect**, an AI-powered application that helps users design, visualize, and optimize intelligent workflows for business processes. The application enables users to create workflow pipelines, automate repetitive tasks, identify bottlenecks, and improve operational efficiency through AI-driven recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the fundamentals of workflow automation.
* Design end-to-end AI-powered workflows.
* Visualize process flow and task dependencies.
* Identify workflow bottlenecks and optimization opportunities.
* Learn how AI enhances business process automation.
* Generate actionable workflow improvement recommendations.
