# Day 50 – Build Defend Your Experience

## AI-Powered Adaptive Interview Defense Simulator

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 50** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built **Defend Your Experience**, an **AI-powered Adaptive Interview Defense Simulator** that prepares candidates for real-world interviews by simulating dynamic interviewer behavior. The application evaluates responses, challenges claims, asks follow-up questions, and provides personalized feedback to help users confidently explain their experience, projects, technical skills, and career decisions.

Unlike traditional mock interview tools, this simulator adapts its questions based on the user's previous responses, creating a realistic interview experience that improves communication, critical thinking, and confidence.

# ⚙ Workflow

1. Read the provided learning resources.
2. Watch the solution video.
3. Open Claude.
4. Set Claude effort level to **Low**.
5. Start a new conversation.
6. Paste the **Defend Your Experience** prompt.
7. Answer Claude's interview setup questions.
8. Generate the complete HTML application.
9. Save the generated HTML file.
10. Open the application locally in your browser.
11. Select your interview mode.
12. Answer adaptive technical and HR interview questions.
13. Respond to follow-up questions based on your previous answers.
14. Defend your projects, skills, and experience with supporting examples.
15. Review AI-generated feedback after each interview round.
16. Analyze your performance dashboard and confidence score.
17. Replay interviews to improve communication and response quality.
18. Capture screenshots of your interview sessions and final report.
19. Upload the HTML file, screenshots, and documentation to your GitHub repository.
