# Day 16 – Build Your First Stock Research Skill

## Overview

On Day 16 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I learned how to use Claude AI to perform stock research and analyze companies before making investment decisions.

Many beginners invest based on social media trends, recommendations, or emotions. However, successful investing starts with proper research. Claude can act as a research assistant, helping investors understand businesses, financial metrics, risks, and growth opportunities.


# Objective

Build a stock research workflow that helps evaluate companies using AI-powered analysis.

The goal is to:

* Understand how a company makes money
* Analyze business fundamentals
* Identify strengths and weaknesses
* Evaluate growth potential
* Assess investment risks
* Make more informed decisions

# What is AI-Powered Stock Research?

AI-powered stock research involves using Claude to analyze publicly available information and generate structured insights about a company.

Instead of spending hours reading reports, investors can use AI to summarize and explain complex information in a more accessible format.


# Key Areas to Research

## 1. Company Overview

Questions to Ask:

* What does the company do?
* How does it generate revenue?
* Who are its customers?
* What industry does it operate in?

Example:

Company: Apple

Research Questions:

* What are Apple's main revenue streams?
* What products contribute most to revenue?
* What gives Apple a competitive advantage?

## 2. Business Model Analysis

Understand:

* Revenue sources
* Pricing strategy
* Customer base
* Competitive positioning

Example Prompt:

Explain Apple's business model in simple terms and identify its competitive advantages.


## 3. Financial Health

Evaluate:

* Revenue growth
* Profitability
* Cash flow
* Debt levels
* Margins

Questions:

* Is the company profitable?
* Is revenue growing consistently?
* Does the company have healthy cash reserves?


## 4. Competitive Analysis

Research:

* Main competitors
* Market position
* Industry leadership
* Competitive moat

Example Prompt:

Analyze Apple's position compared to Samsung, Microsoft, and Google.


## 5. Risk Assessment

Identify:

* Industry risks
* Regulatory challenges
* Economic factors
* Technology disruption risks

Example Questions:

* What are the biggest risks facing the company?
* What could negatively impact future growth?


# 📝 Sample Stock Research Prompt

Act as a professional equity research analyst.

Analyze [Company Name] and provide:

1. Company overview
2. Business model analysis
3. Revenue sources
4. Financial strengths
5. Financial weaknesses
6. Competitive advantages
7. Major risks
8. Growth opportunities
9. Long-term outlook
10. Investment summary

Present the information in a clear and structured format suitable for beginner investors.

# Important Note

AI-generated stock research should be used as a starting point, not as financial advice.

Always verify information using:

* Company annual reports
* Earnings reports
* Official investor relations websites
* Trusted financial sources

Never make investment decisions based solely on AI output.
