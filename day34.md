# Day 34 – Become a Marketing Detective

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 34** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Marketing Detective** application that analyzes marketing campaigns, uncovers customer insights, evaluates competitor strategies, and identifies opportunities to improve brand performance. The simulator demonstrates how AI can assist marketers in making data-driven decisions through campaign analysis, audience behavior, and strategic recommendations.

1. Read the provided learning resources.
2. Watch the solution video.
3. Open Claude.
4. Set Claude effort level to **Low**.
5. Start a new conversation.
6. Paste the **Marketing Detective** prompt.
7. Generate the complete HTML application.
8. Save the generated HTML file.
9. Open the application in your browser.
10. Review the generated business case.
11. Analyze customer personas and audience behavior.
12. Investigate competitor marketing strategies.
13. Evaluate campaign performance metrics.
