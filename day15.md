# Day 15 – Build a Personal Life Analysis Consultant

## Overview

On Day 15 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I learned how to create a **Personal Life Analysis Consultant** using Claude AI.

This AI consultant acts as a personal advisor that helps analyze different aspects of life, identify strengths and weaknesses, uncover growth opportunities, and provide actionable recommendations for improvement.

The goal is to gain clarity, make better decisions, and create a roadmap for personal and professional growth.


##  Objective

Build an AI-powered consultant that can evaluate various areas of life and provide meaningful insights.

Areas of analysis include:

* Career Growth
* Education & Learning
* Productivity
* Time Management
* Financial Habits
* Health & Wellness
* Relationships
* Goal Setting
* Personal Development


## What is a Personal Life Analysis Consultant?

A Personal Life Analysis Consultant is an AI assistant that reviews your current situation, asks thoughtful questions, identifies patterns, and provides recommendations to help you improve your life.

Instead of giving generic advice, it analyzes your unique circumstances and creates personalized suggestions.


## Areas of Analysis

1. Career & Professional Growth

Evaluate:

* Current skills
* Career goals
* Learning progress
* Job readiness
* Networking efforts

Example Questions:

* What skills should I focus on next?
* Am I on track for my target career?
* What gaps exist in my roadmap?


2. Learning & Education

Analyze:

* Learning habits
* Study consistency
* Knowledge gaps
* Skill development

Recommendations:

* Courses to take
* Projects to build
* Topics to prioritize


3. Productivity Analysis

Evaluate:

* Daily routines
* Time management
* Focus levels
* Task completion rate

Identify:

* Productivity blockers
* Time-wasting activities
* Improvement opportunities


4. Goal Tracking

Assess:

* Short-term goals
* Long-term goals
* Progress indicators

Create:

* Action plans
* Milestones
* Accountability systems


5. Health & Lifestyle

Review:

* Sleep habits
* Exercise routines
* Stress levels
* Work-life balance

Provide suggestions for maintaining a healthier lifestyle.


##  Sample Prompt

Act as an experienced Life Coach, Career Consultant, and Productivity Expert.

Analyze the following information about my life:

1. Current situation
2. Goals
3. Daily habits
4. Challenges
5. Strengths
6. Weaknesses

Then provide:

* A life assessment score out of 10
* Strengths analysis
* Areas for improvement
* Risks and challenges
* Actionable recommendations
* A 30-day improvement plan

[Insert Personal Information Here]
