# Day 36 – Explore Your Thinking Patterns

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 36** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Thinking Patterns Explorer** that helps users understand how they approach decisions, solve problems, and respond to different situations. Through interactive scenarios, reflective exercises, and AI-generated insights, the application analyzes thinking styles and provides personalized recommendations to improve critical thinking, decision-making, and self-awareness.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Explore different thinking and decision-making styles.
* Develop greater self-awareness through interactive scenarios.
* Identify strengths and areas for improvement in problem-solving.
* Learn how biases and assumptions influence decisions.
* Use AI-generated insights to support reflective thinking.
* Strengthen critical thinking and analytical skills.

# ⚙ Workflow

1. Read the provided learning resources.
2. Watch the solution video.
3. Open Claude.
4. Set Claude effort level to **Low**.
5. Start a new conversation.
6. Paste the **Explore Your Thinking Patterns** prompt.
7. Generate the complete HTML application.
8. Save the generated HTML file.
9. Open the application in your browser.
10. Read the introduction and assessment instructions.
11. Complete the interactive thinking assessment.
12. Respond to scenario-based decision challenges.
13. Review your identified thinking patterns.
14. Analyze AI-generated insights and recommendations.
15. Observe updates to the Thinking Profile Dashboard.
16. Study the final Thinking Report.
17. Replay the assessment with different scenarios.
18. Capture screenshots of your results.
19. Upload the HTML file, screenshots, and documentation to your GitHub repository.
