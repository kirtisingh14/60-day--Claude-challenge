# Day 32 – Think Like a Marketing Strategist

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 32** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Marketing Strategy Assistant** that helps analyze a business, identify target audiences, evaluate competitors, and develop effective marketing strategies. The application demonstrates how AI can support strategic decision-making by generating actionable marketing plans, campaign ideas, and performance insights.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the fundamentals of marketing strategy.
* Analyze business goals and customer needs.
* Identify target audiences and market segments.
* Evaluate competitors and industry positioning.
* Develop AI-assisted marketing campaigns.
* Measure marketing performance using key metrics.

# ⚙ Workflow

1. Read the provided learning resources.
2. Watch the solution video.
3. Open Claude.
4. Set Claude effort level to **Low**.
5. Start a new conversation.
6. Paste the **Think Like a Marketing Strategist** prompt.
7. Generate the complete React HTML application.
8. Save the generated HTML file.
9. Open the application in your browser.
10. Review the generated business profile.
11. Analyze the target audience.
12. Study competitor insights.
13. Build a marketing strategy.
14. Create AI-powered campaign ideas.
15. Allocate marketing budget across channels.
16. Monitor KPI changes and campaign performance.
17. Review AI-generated recommendations.
18. Analyze the final marketing dashboard.
19. Replay with different business scenarios.
