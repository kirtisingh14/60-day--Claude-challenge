# Day 31 – Build an AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## Overview

Welcome to **Day 31** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Supply Chain Control Tower** that provides end-to-end visibility across the supply chain. The application monitors suppliers, inventory, production, logistics, warehouses, and customer deliveries in real time, enabling businesses to detect disruptions early and make faster, data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the concept of a Supply Chain Control Tower.
* Monitor end-to-end supply chain operations.
* Track key performance indicators (KPIs) in real time.
* Identify operational risks and bottlenecks.
* Improve supply chain resilience using AI insights.
* Support proactive decision-making through intelligent recommendations.
