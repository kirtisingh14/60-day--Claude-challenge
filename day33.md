# Day 33 – Build a Media Integrity Analyzer

## 📌 Overview

Welcome to **Day 33** of my **60 Days of Claude AI Mastery Challenge**.

In this project, I built an **AI-powered Media Integrity Analyzer** that helps evaluate the credibility and reliability of digital media content. The application analyzes news articles, headlines, images, and social media posts to identify potential misinformation, assess source credibility, and provide evidence-based insights for responsible decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the importance of media literacy and information verification.
* Analyze the credibility of digital content using AI.
* Detect potential misinformation and misleading claims.
* Evaluate source reliability and supporting evidence.
* Improve critical thinking through structured media analysis.
* Generate transparent AI-powered integrity reports.
