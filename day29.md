# Day 29 – Build Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## 📌 Overview

On Day 29 of the **60 Days Claude AI Mastery Challenge**, I built **Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab** using Claude AI.

Supply chain disruptions can significantly impact industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, logistics, and retail. This project simulates real-world supply chain crises and demonstrates how AI can help identify risks, optimize resources, prioritize deliveries, and improve decision-making during emergencies.

The simulator provides an interactive dashboard where users analyze supply chain bottlenecks, monitor inventory, manage transportation, and develop crisis response strategies.

---

# 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered Supply Chain Crisis Simulator that helps users:

* Monitor supply chain operations

# 🚚 What is Operation Lifeline?

Operation Lifeline is an AI-powered simulation that recreates supply chain disruptions and challenges users to make strategic decisions to ensure the continuous flow of critical supplies.

The simulator demonstrates how organizations respond to unexpected events while balancing cost, efficiency, and customer needs.
