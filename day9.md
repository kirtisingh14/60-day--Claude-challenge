Day 9/60 – Claude AI Mastery Challenge
Build & Enhance an AI Nutrition Analytics App

Overview
Today, I explored how AI can be used to create intelligent health and nutrition applications by building and enhancing a complete AI Nutrition Analytics Dashboard called NutriScope.

The goal was to transform food logging into a smart, data-driven experience that not only tracks nutrition but also provides actionable health insights and personalized recommendations.

This project demonstrated how Claude Artifacts can be used to rapidly build real-world software products from natural language prompts.

📚 What I Learned
🔹 What is NutriScope?

NutriScope is an AI-powered nutrition intelligence platform that helps users:

Track daily food intake
Monitor calories and nutrients
Analyze nutritional deficiencies
Identify dietary risks
Generate personalized recommendations
Plan healthier meals

Instead of simply counting calories, the application provides a complete nutritional health assessment.

🎯 Why This Project Matters

Nutrition is one of the most important factors affecting long-term health.

Traditional food trackers focus on calorie counting.

AI-powered nutrition systems can:

✅ Analyze nutrient quality

✅ Detect deficiencies

✅ Suggest food improvements

✅ Generate meal plans

✅ Improve dietary awareness

✅ Support healthier decision-making
